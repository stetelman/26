HOW TO PLAY

TYPESET

Play a valid 3–7 letter word.

Every new letter you use fills that key for you.

Letters in the immediately previous word are locked for one turn. You may still use them, but they do not score.

No word may be repeated during the same game.

First player to collect A–Z wins.

PASS & PLAY supports 2–4 players on one phone.
